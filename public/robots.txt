# SteamCanvas: https://previewartwork.lannahirave.com
#
# Preview your real Steam profile before changing anything on Steam. Load any public
# profile and try new artwork, showcases, avatar, avatar frame, background, theme and
# level, then export the showcase images (and a full preview) as a ZIP.
#
# What this version supports:
# - Artwork, Featured Artwork, Screenshot and Workshop showcases (Workshop as 1x5, 2x5
#   or 3x5), split from one image or placed as ready-made panels, GIFs included.
# - Steam Artwork Studio integration (https://artworkstudio.lannahirave.com): GIFs you
#   convert there are saved to its Library in your browser and can be placed into a
#   showcase here, or sent straight over with "Preview on my Steam profile".
# - Community Market and Points Shop backgrounds, avatar frames and profile themes.
# - Drafts saved in your browser, so a reload restores your session.
# - No ads, no account, nothing is changed on Steam.
#
# SteamCanvas is by dumbapplee (https://github.com/dumbapplee/steamcanvas), modified by
# lannahirave (https://github.com/lannahirave/steamcanvas) to work with Steam Artwork
# Studio. AGPL-3.0.
#
# /api/ is the profile and catalogue backend the app calls; it has nothing to index.
